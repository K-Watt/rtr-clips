# rtr_clips

rtr_clips is a YouTube Shorts fan channel for GTA 6 moments from streamers: https://www.youtube.com/@rtr_clips

GTA Clipper is the channel owner's personal tool for uploading the channel's Shorts and replying to comments on them through the YouTube API. Only the channel owner uses it.
